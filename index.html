FAQ

How old do I need to be?

All Panic At The Emo club nights are 18+. Please bring valid photo ID, as venues will check it on the door.

Where do I buy tickets?

Every night on this page links straight to our official ticket page on Skiddle. Tap "Get tickets" on the night you want. Advance tickets are cheaper than paying on the door.

Can I buy on the door?

Usually yes, if the night hasn't sold out, but door tickets cost more. Lots of our nights sell out, so we always recommend buying in advance.

Do I need to dress up?

Not at all, but a lot of people do. Skinny jeans, band tees, eyeliner and side fringes are all very welcome.

I want Panic At The Emo at my venue or event

We'd love to hear from you. Email panicattheemoclubnight@gmail.com with your venue and dates.
